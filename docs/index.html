Rounds — Boxing Timer

A boxing-style interval round timer, stripped to what matters: set it, start it, train to the bell. The timer keeps running with your screen locked. No account, no ads, nothing tracked.

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Use Contact

What it does

  • Set your round count, round length and rest — or tap a preset like Full Card (12 × 3:00).
  • Non-Stop mode for continuous rounds with no fixed end.
  • A bell at the start and end of every round; a wooden clap ten seconds before the bell.
  • Vibration on every cue. Plays over your music, and can dim it while a cue sounds.
  • Runs in the background so the bell rings with the phone locked.
  • Light and dark, following your system.

Rounds Pro

A one-time purchase — not a subscription — that adds activity history and totals, a calendar of your training, Apple Health export, and a shareable workout card. The timer above stays free.
