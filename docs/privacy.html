Rounds does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and the app works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

  • Your workout setup — round count, round and rest lengths — and your preferences (theme, audio settings).
  • Your activity history: each finished workout Rounds records (date, length, rounds completed) and the totals shown on the History screen.

All of this is stored only in your device's local storage. It never leaves your device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you remove the app. Rounds does not back it up to any server or sync it between devices.

Purchases

Rounds Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Rounds never sees your name, payment card, or Apple Account details — it only receives a yes/no answer from the system about whether you own Rounds Pro. Apple's Privacy Policy covers the transaction.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Rounds writes each finished workout to the Health app on your device (workout type, start and end time, duration). It only writes — it never reads any health or fitness data. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app, or in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Background audio

Rounds plays audio in the background so the round bell can ring while your screen is locked. This uses no data and no network.
