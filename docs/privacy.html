Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Rounds does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connection — the app works entirely offline.
  • Your workout setup (round count, round and rest lengths) and preferences (theme, audio settings) are stored only in your device's local storage. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

The app plays audio in the background so the round bell can ring while your screen is locked. This uses no data and no network.
